# Thumbnail idea: Atmos Enemy Range Visualizer

Concept: top-down view of an enemy base with colored range circles and lines pointing to the player.

Compose with: `py -3 scripts/make_thumbnail.py <art> atmos_enemy_range_visualizer`

## Prompt

Square artwork in Factorio style, top-down view of dark alien ground with an enemy outpost: a biter worm emerging from the ground and a gun turret. Around each enemy a translucent glowing range circle with a crisp outline: red circle around the worm, orange circle around the turret, a large faint purple arc of artillery range at the edge. A small engineer character at the lower part of the image, with thin bright colored lines running from the engineer to each enemy. Dark background, vivid neon circles, high contrast, readable at small size, no text, no frame.
