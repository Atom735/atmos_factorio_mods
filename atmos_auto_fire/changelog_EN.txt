Date: 2026-11-06

Date: 2026-11-06
  Bugfixes:
    - Pellet spread now matches the held shotgun: one hidden turret per shotgun with its range and damage modifier.

    - Shotgun shell damage research now applies to auto-fire shots.

Version: 1.1.1
Date: 2026-11-06
  Bugfixes:
    - Hidden turret now fires from a short standoff so shotgun pellets do not spawn inside the player.
    - Proxy shotgun ammo damages enemies only, with a safety heal for same-force / friendly hits.
    - Clears the turret-out-of-ammo alert raised by the temporary one-round magazine.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.1.0
Date: 2026-11-06
  Features:
    - Allow list with per-row target filter: all enemies, entity type, or a specific entity.
    - Each allow row has a range slider in meters or percent of the held weapon range.
    - Forbid list of entities that are never targeted and that block the line of fire.
  Changes:
    - Default allow order: all at 10%, spawners, combat robots, worms, spitters, biters, then all at 100%.
    - Default forbid list: gun turret, laser turret, flamethrower turret.
    - Avoid-own-buildings line-of-fire toggle stays on by default.
  Gui:
    - Settings window rebuilt for allow/forbid lists, entity pickers, and m/% switches.
---------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Version: 1.0.0
Date: 2026-11-06
  Features:
    - Automatic shotgun fire through a hidden turret that stays under the player.
    - Shots spend ammo from the equipped gun slot and then lock the weapon cooldown.
    - Target order: panic enemies, behemoth worms, other worms, spawners, then other units.
    - Optional cone check so a shot is skipped when a friendly structure is in the spread.
  Gui:
    - Top-bar button opens the settings window for panic radius and priority order.
    - Shortcut bar button toggles auto-fire and shows a flying status message.
  Locale:
    - English and Russian.
