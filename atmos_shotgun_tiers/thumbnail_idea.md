# Thumbnail idea: Atmos Shotgun Tiers

Concept: tier progression of shotgun shells, each tier spraying more pellets.

Compose with: `py -3 scripts/make_thumbnail.py <art> atmos_shotgun_tiers`

## Prompt

Square artwork in Factorio industrial painterly style, dark smoky background with warm orange lighting. Three shotgun shells standing in a row from left to right, growing in size and quality: a plain red shell, a brass-capped green shell, a glowing purple high-tier shell. From the top of each shell a fan of glowing pellets bursts upward, each fan visibly denser than the previous one (few, more, many pellets). Small subtle upward chevrons hint at tiers. Strong silhouette, high contrast, readable at small size, centered composition with margin, no text, no frame.
