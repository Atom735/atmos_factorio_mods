# Thumbnail idea: Atmos Auto Heal

Concept: automatic healing from inventory items, ordered by priority.

Compose with: `py -3 scripts/make_thumbnail.py <art> atmos_auto_heal`

## Prompt

Square artwork in Factorio industrial painterly style, dark metallic background. Center: a large glowing green cross / heart health symbol with a circular automation arrow wrapped around it. Below it three inventory slots in a row labeled only by icons: a raw fish, a healing capsule, a medkit, with small glowing priority markers above them (1, 2, 3 as simple dots of decreasing brightness). Soft green healing particles rising. High contrast, readable at small size, centered with margin, no text, no frame.
